version=1
title=Continued TV Broadcast: Life and Living
description=[h1]Life and Living TV, now in endless reruns[/h1]
description=In vanilla, Life and Living TV (channel 203) airs its cooking, carpentry, fishing, foraging and other how-to shows for the first 9 days, then turns to static and goes silent for good.
description=
description=This mod keeps it on the air. When the last episode ends on day 9, the full schedule starts over from episode 1 the next day, every 10 days, for the rest of your run.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Same shows, same times, same XP and recipes as vanilla, repeated forever.
description=[*]Reruns grant skill XP again, still limited by the vanilla [i]Media XP Level Cutoff[/i] sandbox option. Or turn off [i]Reruns Give XP Again[/i] so each character only gets XP from each show the first time they watch it.
description=[*]Safe to add mid-game: if the channel already went off the air in your save, reruns start from episode 1 the day you load it.
description=[*]Sandbox options: rerun [b]back to back[/b] (with optional days off air between runs) or [b]monthly[/b] (the full schedule from the 1st of every in-game month, then off air until the next 1st).
description=[*]Singleplayer and multiplayer (server-side). No game files replaced, so it should keep working across game updates.
description=[/list]
description=
description=You still need a powered TV (or a generator once the power goes out).
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Build 42. Doesn't replace RadioData.xml, so it works alongside other radio/TV mods. Mods that rewrite the Life and Living channel itself may conflict.
